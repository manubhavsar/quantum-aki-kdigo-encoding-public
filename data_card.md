## Inclusion / exclusion

**Include** a unit stay if it has >=2 valid serum creatinine measurements (needed for any
rate-of-change feature). **Include** a prediction point at t in {12,24,...,96} h if:
the stay is still active at t; >=2 creatinine values fall in [0, t]; and no AKI onset has
occurred at or before t (we predict *incident* AKI).

**Exclude** creatinine outside [0.1, 20] mg/dL, BUN outside [1, 200] mg/dL, weight outside
(20, 300] kg. A prediction point is **censored (label = NaN, dropped per-horizon)** when the
patient is discharged before the horizon closes and no event was observed.

## Labelling

Primary label is **creatinine-only KDIGO stage >=1** (`src/kdigo.py`):
C1 rise >=0.3 mg/dL vs the trailing-48 h minimum; C2 >=1.5x the trailing-7 d minimum.

The urine criterion (C3, <0.5 mL/kg/h for 6 h) is implemented and reported as a
**sensitivity analysis only**. Diagnostic check on eICU: 51% of 6 h urine bins contain a
single charted entry (median inter-entry gap 120 min), and bins with >=2 entries show
18.9% oliguria vs 24.4% across all bins — i.e. sparse charting inflates apparent oliguria.
Including C3 nearly doubles the stay-level event rate
(25.4% -> 49.6%), which we judge to be
label noise rather than signal. Creatinine-only KDIGO is also standard in comparable
MIMIC/eICU AKI-ML studies.

## Features (13)

Shared demographics (3): `age`, `sex_male`, `weight_kg`.

**Static block (5)** — level-based, mirroring static CKD-QML encodings:
`cr_last`, `cr_mean`, `cr_max`, `bun_last`, `bun_mean`.

**Delta block (5)** — KDIGO-aligned rate-of-change:
`cr_delta_48h` (the KDIGO C1 quantity), `cr_ratio_7d` (the KDIGO C2 quantity),
`cr_rate_per_h`, `bun_delta`, `bun_rate_per_h`.

Both blocks derive from the *same two laboratory series*, differing only in level vs.
rate-of-change, so the static-vs-delta contrast is controlled rather than confounded.

Note: `cr_delta_48h` and `cr_ratio_7d` are bounded above by the KDIGO thresholds
(0.3 mg/dL and 1.5x) *by construction*, since any point exceeding them would already be
labelled AKI and excluded. Models therefore must detect **sub-threshold** trajectories.

## Leakage control

Splits are made on **patient identifier** (`uniquepid` / `subject_id`), so every
prediction point and every unit stay belonging to a patient falls entirely within one
split. eICU contains multiple stays per patient
(2058 stays from 1609 patients), so row-level splitting
would leak. Prediction points after a patient's AKI onset are dropped, so no post-event
information enters the features.

## Known limitations

- Demo-scale cohorts, not the full eICU/MIMIC databases (no credentialing in the timeline).
- MIMIC-IV demo external cohort is small (98 patients); its estimates carry wide intervals.
- Urine-output criterion unreliable at this charting density; primary labels are creatinine-only.
- eICU is US-only; generalisation beyond that setting is untested.
